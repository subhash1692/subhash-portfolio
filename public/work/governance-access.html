THE CHALLENGE

Start with the problem.

Cloud permissions, workspace membership, compute entitlements, storage access, and data grants are separate control planes. Misalignment leads to fragile onboarding and hard-to-diagnose production failures.

MY ENGINEERING SCOPE

From decisions to delivery.

I design domain and environment boundaries, automate Unity Catalog grants and related Azure permissions, and develop controlled provisioning and onboarding workflows for engineers, analysts, and data scientists.

DESIGN DECISIONS

How I approach it.

01

Separate access concerns

Different identities and entitlements for deployment, workload execution, administration, and data consumption.

02

Review grants as code

Catalog objects, external locations, and privileges are managed with idempotent, auditable scripts.

03

Enable bounded self-service

Reusable templates and approval-aware workflows allow routine requests without persistent elevated access.

04

Verify the real outcome

Access is tested as the intended user or service—not inferred from the success of a prior command.
